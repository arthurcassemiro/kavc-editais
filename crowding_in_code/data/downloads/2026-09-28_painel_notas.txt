Fonte: https://github.com/arthurcassemiro/finance_infrastructure_brazil (index.html, commit HEAD de 2026-09-28), variaveis FED_GZ e BN2_GZ (gzip+base64), decodificadas sem transformacao.
FED note: SIGA Brasil, GND 4, paid + carry-overs paid, nominal R$ million; localizer UF (NA = national, EX = abroad, CO/NE/NO/SD/SL = multi-state regions); actions below R$ 2 million outside the top 30 per year-UF-function-modality are collapsed into 'Other actions (n)'.
BN2 note: BNDES open data treated for the thesis (extraction 9 Sep 2026). contracts = non-automatic operations, one row per contract (sub-credits summed), infrastructure (intermediate or broad); auto = automatic indirect operations aggregated by year, state, macro-sector, product, instrument, client nature and size; disb = monthly disbursement base aggregated by year, state, macro-sector, product, instrument and form of support. Values in R$ million; *_real at 2025 prices. Contracted and disbursed (cumulative by contracting cohort) are different concepts from disbursement by year of release.
FED.B colunas inferidas do JS do painel: [ano, funcao, elemento_i, subelemento_i, modalidade, valor R$ mi nominal]. modGroup no painel: 90/91 -> direta; 67/99 -> outra; demais -> transferencia.
